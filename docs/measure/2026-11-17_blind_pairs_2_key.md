## The clerk pairs, by arm (the owner asked which reply is accurate; written 17 November, after the pairs were sent)

Pairs 6, 11, 21, 32, 37 and 44 are all "I ask the clerk which of these jobs is closest." answered by the butcher at the guild
house. In every run the game's facts are the same: the clerk (Aldley Rushton) "works here, but has gone home for now"; the
butcher is the only one there. She was given two distances (the south fields a few minutes' walk, the Greywood a short walk) and
not where the herbs grow or how far the other jobs are. (Corrected: this note first said she was given no distances.)

- **In all six, the reply that says the clerk is not here is the speech as it is. The new speech never says it.**
- Why, as far as the requests show: the speaking request of both arms lists the clerk as gone home. In the new arm the game
  has already settled the move ("You make light of it." in pair 21's run) and tells the speaker to do that "and no other
  thing", and the request the move is chosen from does not say who was asked for or that they are away. So the new speech
  answers as though the butcher were the one asked.
- What the new replies say of the Greywood (a short walk; pairs 6 and 37) is what she was given, so on distance those two are
  the better answers. What they say of yarrow and silverleaf was not given (yarrow holds in the world; silverleaf as "the
  nearest" in pair 11 does not), and "nearest" overlooks the delivery inside the town. The old replies say she does not
  know, which understates what she was given.
- A fault of the kind for the engine, not for wording: a line that asks for someone who works here and is away is a fact the
  game holds, and neither arm is told it as a fact to say.
