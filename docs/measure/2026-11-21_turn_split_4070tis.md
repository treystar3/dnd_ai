# Where a turn's seconds go, on the owner's card (2026-11-21)

One run of the live gate's draft script (data/replay/gate_script.yaml, 42 lines: look, explore, walk, a fight, camp, talk) through
tools/nightrun.py on the real model, seed 719, a level-1 warrior with a party of two, at 350ce31. The card is the owner's RTX 4070
Ti Super (16 GB), not the 3060 the game is built for. Its logs were kept outside the project (MERAVIA_LOGS in a scratch folder).

How far to trust it: one run, early game, a short script. Talk times vary by about a quarter between runs that say the same words,
and townsfolk prompts in later play are larger (the nights' p95 is 36,345 characters).

## The card

| | Read (tokens a second) | Written (tokens a second) |
|---|---:|---:|
| RTX 4070 Ti Super, this run | 2,043 | 88 |
| RTX 3060, the approach review's figures (scaled from an A4000) | 757 | 31.7 |

The 4070 Ti Super reads and writes about 2.7 to 2.8 times as fast. Scaled by that, the turns below would take roughly 17 s at the
median and 43 s at the 95th percentile on a 3060 (an estimate, not measured).

## Turns

All 42 turns: median 6.3 s, 95th percentile 15.6 s, none over the 60 s limit. The first word arrives at about the end of the turn
(nothing streams). Model calls are 95% of a turn's time; the engine's own work is about 1%.

One populate_place call took 131 s of wall time against 8.9 s on the server. It was off the turn's path (the turns' own seconds do
not hold it); why it waited is not known.

## What it says

- **Writing costs more than reading.** Streaming narration (doc 09 B3) cuts the largest share of the wait. Narration alone is 27% of
  all turn seconds, three quarters of it writing.
- **Reading a line is cheap in time.** Its 12,000 characters of rules are mostly cached (76%): about 1 s a line here. Cutting them is
  for the quality of the reading, not for speed.
- **A townsperson's prompt is read almost whole every time** (4% reused). That is where a shorter prompt, and fixed text placed
  first, saves time: about 1.5 s a reply here, about 4 s on a 3060.
- On this card no turn came near the limit. The 3060 is where time matters.
