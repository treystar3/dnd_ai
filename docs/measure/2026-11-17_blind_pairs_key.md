## The maker's own reading of the twenty pairs (not blind)

I made the pairs, so I have seen the key. For what it is worth, logged as the standing playtest note asks (does speech read
as a person's or a machine's):

- **Preferred:** the new reply in 8 pairs, the old in 5, no preference in 7. Of the pairs with a preference that is 62%; of all
  twenty it is 40%. By my reading the bar is not clearly met and not clearly missed.
- **Where the new speech is better:** it is shorter and plainer, and it loses two habits of the old that read as a machine's. One
  is the stiff register ("The clerk is not here at this time", "closer than my current workspace allows"). The other is the
  companion who answers a confidence with a counsellor's question ("Why do you think that loss stayed with you so much longer
  than your wins? You sound like you still feel some sting from it."); the new reply to the same line is "A mistake anyone
  could make."
- **Where the new speech is worse:**
  - *The move "turn to what weighs on you" walks away from the question.* Asked who made a knife, and asked to sharpen a blade,
    the flour seller answers with the town's dependence on the trade road and never answers what was asked. The same worry, in
    nearly the same words, came up in her replies in all three runs. The old replies to those lines answered and were
    useful (where the nearest smith is, and how far).
  - *The quiet move cuts a reply that was showing something.* A hurt companion asked how they are holding up: old "I'm fine. Just
    need a minute for my hands to stop shaking so much.", new "I'm fine."
  - *The ask-back move gives a stock question.* "What brings folks like yourself through Parth today?" after a price was asked,
    three times. On a companion it came out as a stranger's question in run 1 ("what brings a man of your standing into these
    parts?").
  - *A reading that picks a name to give can leave the name as the whole reply.* The innkeeper, asked for the town's news,
    answered only "The name is Ingmer Millcombe." (run 1, not among the pairs).
- **Machine or person:** by my count a machine's register shows in 6 of the 20 old replies and 5 of the 20 new ones. The new
  ones are of a different kind: not stiff, but beside the point (the road worry) or oddly put ("There isn't a forge here to have
  any age", "those specific losses").
- **What this points at, if the owner keeps decide then speak:** the weights and words of three moves (own trouble, quiet, ask
  back) rather than the design. They are data (data/rules/reply.yaml), the owner's numbers and words, and a first proposal.

## The reply the gate counted as a leak

Asked the price of a waterskin, with the switch on: "I don't sell those here. What brings folks like yourself through Parth
today?" It says nothing of the news; the counter matched it to the sickness in the flocks of Parth by "through" and "Parth".
