Beside you, Frib Sprocketcrank keeps his robes dry with a small flick of his fingers, while Calsa and Dynn maintain a steady pace, their boots sinking slightly into the saturated loam. The afternoon light is muffled by the overcast sky, turning the vibrant greens of the spring growth into a subdued, weeping olive.

_On the road - The Seeker's Burden_

Huddled under the drooping branches of a massive willow tree, a man in a sodden, heavy traveling cloak sits on a stone. He holds a stack of parchment sealed with a wax stamp from the noble houses of Riverhaven. Beside him, a small wooden chest sits in the mud, its lid slightly ajar. He looks up as you approach, his face weary and his eyes rimmed with red from crying; he is clutching a small, hand-carved wooden doll and a bundle of silk ribbons. He doesn't move to stand, only watching you with a desperate, hollow-eyed expression.

_You stop here, on the road. Onward to Willowmead, or back to Riverhaven._

Rain falls in a persistent mist over the River Road, turning the path into a slick ribbon of gray sludge that clings to your iron sabatons. You move north toward the Willowmead wetlands, the landscape opening up as the noise of Riverhaven fades behind you. No beasts leap from the brush, nor do bandits emerge from the shadows; instead, nature offers only its damp indifference. Rainwater pools in ruts carved by wagon wheels, and clumps of moss cling like velvet pillows to the trunks of trees lining the route.

You find no immediate prey for your blade along this stretch. Instead, you pass twisted roots rising out of the mire and notice common sights: a discarded piece of rotted timber caught in a bramble bush, several smooth river stones polished white by constant flow, and occasional feathers washed up on the muddy bank nearby. To one side, a massive willow tree bows low beneath the weight of moisture. Beneath its drooping canopy sits a man, his figure draped in a water-logged traveling cloak stiffened by mud. He rests upon a stone, clutching an object: a hand-carved wooden doll intertwined with silk ribbons. A stack of parchment, marked with noble wax seals, lies near him beside a small chest whose lid hangs open towards the muck. As you approach, he lifts his head, eyes rimmed red and features hollowed out with exhaustion. His expression remains fixed in a silent, desperate plea.
