# fight_abilities: Abilities, spells, perks and summons in a fight

This workstream makes what a fight's abilities promise hold for every case of a kind, not for the one case that found each fault. It builds seven pieces of infrastructure: an ability, a blow and an element's effect land only on someone still in the fight; a rout is applied after the action that caused it; a turned foe is on one side to every rule of harm, held there by a fence; a kill outright goes down a declared damage path; what is settled when a two-turn ability is begun reaches the turn it lands; one function knows a price and one takes it; and a lock is not a cooldown. Seven smaller features follow: the Bound summon's bond written back from a fight, a summon casting's own serial, a refusal before the cost for commands, a heal's card built from the engine's numbers, gear's spell power scaled where every roll passes, caps read from data, and the acting fighter with its own dice. Four features stand on fight_core and are not built before it: feature 4 needs FIGHT_CORE-1 (the one fall step) and FIGHT_CORE-3 (the floors table); feature 5 needs FIGHT_CORE-1, whose fall stage is where the rout is rolled; feature 2 is built after FIGHT_CORE-2 and FIGHT_CORE-6, which edit the same lines; and feature 1 shares one predicate, `in_fight`, with FIGHT_CORE-2. Left to the owner: what Overcast and the reset relic may buy through, which refusals of death answer a kill outright, what a dominated foe does when its side is gone, whether a turned foe is an ally for a blessing and whether its old side may strike it, the transfer heal's price, whether a wasted command stands, what the weapon's Echoing is, whether a cache's gear rolls as a drop's does, and every new line a player will read.

## Features

### FIGHT_ABILITIES-1. An ability lands only on a fighter still in the fight, and no blow fells anyone twice

### FIGHT_ABILITIES-5. Who breaks at a death is settled when the action that caused it has finished
- Fixes: fight-abilities-2
- Pillar: 1 A world that is real (no foe is both fled and dead, and none is looted that got away); 5 Writing a reader wants to keep reading (the narrator is handed the blow, the fall, then the rout). Checklist 2, 4.
- Kind of case: any action with a blow or a consequence still to land when a death routs someone: area spells, sweeps, the Sweeping shape, cleaves and follow-throughs, and every effect that draws up its list of victims before its loop (Starfall, a relic's nova, a summon's burst and pulse, Unmaking), in a player's action and in a foe's own turn.
- Build: FIGHT_CORE-1, as its review left it, rolls the rout in its `fall` stage on the damage path at the moment of death, keeps the blow's die where morale rolls it today, and queues nothing (combat.settle_falls, which the earlier draft of this feature was written on, is gone): every rout is told after its fall, but it is still applied in the middle of an area cast, and FIGHT_CORE-1's Touches hands this workstream "an area cast whose kills now rout as they fall". The build: an action's scope, opened by act_ability, act_attack, a foe's turn, an ally's turn, an improvised act and start_turn's ticks, and closed in a finally, so a failed action leaves nothing held. Inside a scope, a break that a death's rout asks for (behaviors.morale_on_death, and morale() when the strongest breaks) rolls its dice where it does now and applies nothing: it holds the fighter, whether it yields, and the line its caller tells ("breaks, seeing X fall", "loses heart seeing X break"), so the story never says a foe broke and then that it was struck. A foe's own choice in its turn (pre_turn's bolt and yield) and a yield at its own floor (damage.yields_break) end that fighter's part at once and are not held. morale_on_death's living and morale()'s rest leave out a held fighter as they leave out one that fled, so the same foes roll at the same odds. When the outermost scope closes, each held break is applied through the one way out (behaviors._break, or FIGHT_CORE-6's function once it is in) and its line told, unless that fighter has fallen since; FIGHT_CORE-6's answer of whether the foe broke waits for this flush, so a caller's line is told only for a break that happened. A scope opened inside another (a perk's repeat, a release) does not flush, or the middle of the outer loop would be opened again. The flush repeats while it causes a fall of its own (No One Escapes strikes a runner and can kill, and that death can rout others); it ends because each pass takes fighters out. Outside any scope a break applies at once. The held breaks live in a private attribute of the Combat model (pydantic PrivateAttr: not dumped, so neither the saved row nor the fingerprint holds it, and simulate's deep copy keeps its own; Combat forbids extra fields, so it cannot be a field), the same home FIGHT_CORE-3's blow in progress needs, so one mechanism serves both. Every fight row is written by the scene or by the service's begin after an action has returned (scenes/combat_scene.py 293, 577, 692, 707, 727, 963; services/fight.py:296), never inside one, so no held break is lost to a save. The loops that draw their victims first (engine/combat.py:2205 and 2380, the sweep at 1896, engine/relics.py:70, engine/summons.py:155-168 and 182-205, engine/combat_perks.py:604-627) then need no test of their own, since nobody leaves by a rout in the middle of an action; FIGHT_ABILITIES-1's guard stands behind them for the dead. A held foe stays a foe until the flush, so a repeat or a carry-on later in the same action (Grave Chorus, Follow Through, Cleave) may strike it, as it may any foe still standing. If FIGHT_CORE-1 is not approved, the same hold goes into today's morale stage and behaviors.morale_on_death, and the Recordings line below changes as it says.
