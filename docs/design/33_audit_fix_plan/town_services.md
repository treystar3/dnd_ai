# town_services: What a character does in a town

This workstream fixes ten faults in what a character does in a town: a rank trial begun by a question, a whole stack of reagent spent on one enchantment, a teacher's fee taken for nothing, the wrong carried thing sold, dropped or thrown away, a yes that goes to the board when a person asked, raw copper worn in a slot named "none", a posting struck from a board that was never saved, a lock-picking ability whose cooldown is checked and never started, and memories that do not say whom they are about. It builds five pieces of infrastructure: one seam every trial of rank starts through, one writer for a thing spent from a stack with a fence on the others, one whole-word resolver for "which carried thing do these words mean" over the pack, the key items and the things in hand, one decision for what a yes answers when a request and a board are both in reach, and one step that spends a field ability used as an edge. The rest are small and each closes its kind at the place the state is written (an offer checked again when it is taken, a slot checked against the list in data, a posting struck where the dungeon is remade, a fence on memories made by hand). It leaves three things to the owner: whether a picklock's edge on a lock starts the ability's cooldown, what the game does when two different carried things fit the words equally, and the reading of a handful of new game notes. Nothing here changes what a model is asked or allowed to write.
