# situations: Situations, acts, events, arcs and jobs

All twenty-two findings stood when the code was opened, so none is refused; they come down to seventeen features. Most are one of five kinds: a decision read from the situation record's 40-row window (who an end credits, what a way met makes true); the act's chain, checked in two loops with two copies of its row builder, so the order, the doer and the roll's kind each have a hole; an arc's captives, names and last fight, where one record answers for another; what a situation's end and its parts leave behind (a scene's stale worries, a condition back in force, stock a ruling called lost); and one job done by several writers (health lost outside a fight, the single-player reads in engine/). Two carry the high weight: accepting an arc on a long save divides by zero once the sixteen captive names are used, and a situation won by its own way can end lost. Eight features hold a choice that is the owner's; each builds as its recommended branch without the answer. One stub check was run for this plan (scratchpad/plan/work/situations/treaty_check.py): a treaty way met on a situation with parts makes no change at all today, and one deed meets only the first matching way.
