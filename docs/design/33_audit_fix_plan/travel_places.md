# travel_places: Travel and places

This workstream makes the places a journey makes hold together: a road stop is joined only to places on the map and leaves the map one way, a walk to somewhere the character already stands gets an answer, and what a dungeon gives or takes back is kept and told. It builds four pieces of infrastructure: one rule for what a road stop is and where it stands on its road (with two new rules in the save's own check, so a way that leads off the map can never be saved quietly again); a shared "already so" answer beside refuse(), which f730's commit asked for and TASK_FAILURE-9 and the dungeon's search lean on; a guard at every open and every waking that stands a character on the map; and one rule for what counts as settled (GOALS.md:227, review Q15: not a road stop, not a dungeon's mouth), which the towns a character remembers ask. Numbered names are read by the whole-name reader the game already has (engine/addressing.py spoken_of), not a new one. All eleven findings are confirmed against the code and placed in a feature; this plan's own check found one more case of the first kind (a landmark walked to from a stop strands the player, with a KeyError on every walk and after a reload), which TRAVEL_PLACES-1 closes, and its review found one more of the fourth (a dungeon's mouth is remembered as a town, and Word of Recall takes the character there), which TRAVEL_PLACES-4 closes. The owner is left five choices: whether a night at a road stop is a last rest, whether the two left-overs of f730 move to the new answer, whether a failed search uses a chamber up, what a remade dungeon does to postings that name it (one answer with TOWN_SERVICES-7), and the order a fork's branches are asked in.
