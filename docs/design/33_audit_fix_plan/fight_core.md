# fight_core: The rules of a fight

This workstream fixes how a fighter goes out of a fight and what follows from it: who still takes a turn, what a death sets off and who is told and credited, which floors hold a killing harm, who decides a trial of rank, and what a step on the battlefield is told as. It builds four pieces of infrastructure the rest hangs on: the line of a blow placed before what the blow set off (FIGHT_CORE-5, built first because the fall step stands on it), one fall step that every damage path ends in (FIGHT_CORE-1), one question "is this fighter in the fight, and has it had its turn" asked at the moment of acting (FIGHT_CORE-2), and one way out of a fight for the living (FIGHT_CORE-6). Around them: the floors declared once for every path and holding the whole blow, a duel decided on its duellists, a step that returns what happened, and one test for "may this fighter leave where it stands". No feature here adds a field to the fight's saved row: the row is hashed whole into the fingerprint of every recorded fight turn (storage/statehash.py), so a new field would move stub_fight and stub_short from end to end. Left to the owner: which floors hold against a tick, whether a summoner may call summons in a trial, which movement abilities break a hold and whether that rule is built now or with phase 4, who is credited for a kill by an affliction, and every new line a player reads. Nothing here changes a prompt, a sampler setting or the turn's budget.

## Features

### FIGHT_CORE-1. A death happens once, whatever killed: one fall step on every damage path releases, tells and credits it
- Fixes: engine2-2, trace-fight-4, trace-fight-5
- Pillar: 1, A world that is real (prose and game state agree); 6, Fun, deep, fair play; infrastructure. Checklist 2, 4, 7, 8.
- Kind of case: every way a fighter loses the last of their health (the blow's own target, side damage from a perk, thorns, a guard's share, a burst, a tick, a torn wound, a rebound, Death's Door, a curse or burning ground at the start of a turn), for a foe, an ally and a player fighter alike.
- Build: engine/damage.py gets one `fall` stage and one function behind it, run on the path at the moment a health-writing stage takes a fighter from standing to zero (the path sees the change itself, so it runs once per death and needs no mark to clear when someone is raised). The kill is credited by one function (kill_round, combat_perks.on_kill, the thrall or the marks that wait for death, Reap, Soul Harvest, Triumph). For the blow's own target it is paid where it is paid today, at the end of _apply_damage: the path hands its Blow back to that one caller and the tail asks whether this blow's path felled the target, so a blow nested in another (the pack bond, engine/combat.py:1576) cannot tell or pay the same death twice.
