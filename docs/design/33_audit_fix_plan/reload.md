# reload: What a close and reopen loses

This workstream makes a game that is closed and opened again play as the same game left open would have. It builds one test that nothing has today (every name the scene holds declares what an opening does with it, and a session played straight is compared with the same session closed and opened part-way), and then moves each thing the audit found living on the scene alone to a home in the save: the campaign's own places on the map, what a saved fight is for, the talk in progress and who spoke last, what waits on the next line after a fight, what the checks and prompts remember of the telling, and two small counters. It also mends the list of saved games (a game's folder says it is a game, the index is only a cache) and the two ways a new game can leave a dead row or erase the wrong folder. Nothing here adds or changes a prompt line; where a reopened session will now ask the model what a straight one asks, the entry says so. Left to the owner: which of the scene's small memories an opening is meant to forget, whether a copied-in save folder is listed, whether the telling's memory is kept across a close at all, and three rules of play met on the way (a moment when the period turns, the cooldown of an ability used outside a fight, whether a brew spends a crafting boost).
