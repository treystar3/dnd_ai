# people_talk: People and talk outside a fight

This workstream mends what a townsperson, a hire or a companion is told about themselves and about the player before they speak: words the engine garbles when it fills in a pronoun or turns a detail into the first person, a memory dated a day late, a "last thing you remember of them" that is about someone else, a wood by Parth handed to contractors in thirteen halls. It builds eight small pieces of infrastructure, each closing a kind: one pronoun fill that touches only lines about the one it fills and never agrees a past form; one reader for "a memory of this character"; a die of its own for each thing rolled about a person; one day base for a companion's memory, taken from the minute; one writer for a companion's bond and a daily bound on a deed's trust; role lines in data with slots the world fills; a pair's line that stays owed until the model has written it; and a deed's verb named once for its three lines. Three features change what a model is asked and are measured on a card before they are kept (PEOPLE_TALK-5, -9 and branch b of -16); the rest are built on the stub suite. Left to the owner: whether a gift moves a companion's bond at all (review Q20 says memory only), the gift numbers and whether Campfire Cook's step is bounded by the day, whether typed hiring stays off, whether Sworn Enemy may name all seventeen kinds of foe, and whether the old reply request is mended before the decide-then-speak switch is turned. Scripts named audit/work/... are in the audit's scratchpad folder; the small read-only probes this plan ran are in plan/work/people_talk/ (the review's: motive_fill_probe.py, pair_seed_probe.py and pair_seed_probe2.py, the last two on copies of the kept saves).

## Features

### PEOPLE_TALK-1. The engine's pronoun fill changes only its own lines, and only the pronoun and the verb it governs
- Fixes: engine3-2
- Pillar: 4 People who feel like people (the same pronoun every time; nothing garbled in what a speaker is told of themselves); checklist 4, 6
- Kind of case: Every line the engine fills with one person's pronoun before a model reads it: a companion's brief, a townsperson's way of talking, a quirk, mood or growth line, a fight line.
- Build: engine/entities.py gendered() becomes one pass over the text as written. It swaps they, them, their, theirs, themselves, and agrees only the word that follows a subject "they" it has just replaced (past its adverbs), never a word after a he, she or it that was already there, and never after an "it" that came from "them". A word in the `verbs` list is agreed; one in `leave` (modals, "cannot", "first", and the past forms: the common irregular pasts of English, not only those in data today) is left as written. A word in neither can only come from text the engine did not write (a hire's details, written by the model, until PEOPLE_TALK-5): it is agreed by its shape as today unless it is a regular past in -ed outside the bare verbs that end so (need, feed, bleed, heed, shed, speed, breed, wed, listed in `verbs`), so "They hum when they clean their gear" still reads "He hums when he cleans his gear" (a closed list alone would hand back "He hum when he clean"). The lists live in a new data/rules/agreement.yaml (`verbs`, `leave`, the irregular forms now in _AGREE, and `filled`: the data files and keys whose lines go through the fill); engine/data_check.py refuses a word listed twice and any word that follows "they" in a `filled` line and is in neither list (about 80 distinct words today, about 25 of them past forms: broke, came, found, said, saw, learned, stopped). The audit adds a wider fault than the grammar: in scenes/banter_actions.py companion_brief the fill runs over the joined text, so a companion's own kept claim "My brothers taught me to fish; they were patient with me" is handed back as "she was patient with me", and "thinks they fuss" said of another companion takes the speaker's pronoun (probe: plan/work/people_talk/gendered_probe.py). So the fill goes line by line: the how-they-speak lines and the role and blurb line as made, and of the records context.select chooses (the brief takes each chosen text back to its record and kind; select's other callers are left as they are), only those the engine writes (concern, origin, trait, and a detail's "About them:" line) are filled, after the choice, so the texts the choice embeds stay as they are; what a person said or what was said of them (kept claims and beliefs, promises held, opinions, views of the others) passes as written. A filled line uses they and them only of the one it is about: two motives in data/rules/party.yaml, filled today as a concern, use "them" of someone else (protect "works to keep them so", the one kept safe; revenge "has not found them yet", the one who wronged them), and the fill turns that into her or him (plan/work/people_talk/motive_fill_probe.py); they are reworded so, the new words read by the owner first (they reach a model only, never the player).
- Test first: tools/sim/pronoun_fill_test.py. Fails today on: "they walked away and they said nothing" (he), "You gave them food, and they keep close." (it), "let it show", "they cannot stand waste", a detail already written with she ("She keeps a notched wooden spoon that she carved"), a brief whose kept claim or opinion holds a "they" that is someone else, and the protect and revenge motives filled for she. Second case: over every string of the `filled` data keys and each of he, she, it, the output differs from the input only at a pronoun or at a listed verb after a replaced "they"; "they pass", "they need", "they carry" still agree; and the stand-in's details (simlib.py a_PersonaDetails) filled for he read "He grew up", "He loves", "He hums when he cleans", as model-written text a list does not hold. Starts from audit/work/engine3_gendered.py and sk_engine3_1_they.py; the three cases of tools/sim/identity_test.py:83-87 stay.
- Recordings: moves a golden: yes. stub_party's hire_chat and exchange requests hold "About him: He grews up in a fishing village"; they will read "He grew up" (the stub's other details read as today). No embed call moves: stub_party's embed calls carry the records' words unfilled ("Their family were...", "About them: They grew up..."), and the choice still reads them so. stub_fight only if one of its briefs holds a filled word, which the replay shows; a recorded hire whose motive is protect or revenge would show the reworded line (none of the five recorded hires has either: coin, atonement, rank).
